Task 4 – Default-Deny Policy

Part C

Task 5 – SSH Config Snapshot

grep -E '^(PermitRootLogin|PasswordAuthentication|PubkeyAuthentication|Port)' /etc/ssh/sshd_config 2>/dev/null || echo "sshd_config not readable"
Part C

Task 6 – Match SSH Hardening

Write A (PermitRootLogin no), B (PasswordAuthentication no), or C (Port 2222):

Eliminates password-guessing attacks against SSH
Prevents attackers from directly targeting the root account
Reduces automated scans that only look for port 22
Part D

Task 7 – Auth Log Review

sudo journalctl -u sshd --no-pager -n 15 2>/dev/null || sudo tail -n 15 /var/log/auth.log
sudo journalctl -u sshd --grep "Failed password" --no-pager -n 5 2>/dev/null || sudo grep "Failed password" /var/log/auth.log | tail -5
Part D

Task 8 – Why Review Auth Logs?

In 2–3 sentences, explain why regularly reviewing authentication logs is essential.
