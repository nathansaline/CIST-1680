Scenario & Objectives

Aligned outcomes: help documentation, redirection/pipes, text processing, resource monitoring, and process control. Your ~/sysop-lab directory is already set up from Formative 1. Work through Parts A–C in the terminal, then click Submit Assessment to check your work. This simulator is for practice — the official worksheet still needs your real output and written answers.

Part A · Getting Help

Task 1 — Practice the main help systems:

ls --help | head -15
man -k copy | head -5
whatis ls
man ls | head -20
VERIFY
From the SYNOPSIS in the man page, write the general syntax pattern for ls, plus the one-line description from whatis.

Task 2 (written, not graded here) — In 1–2 sentences, explain the difference between --help and a full man page, and when you'd use each.

Scratch space only — transfer your real answer to the worksheet.

Part B · Redirection and Pipes

Task 3 — Create a small data set and practice redirection/piping:

ls -la /etc > ~/sysop-lab/etc-listing.txt
wc -l ~/sysop-lab/etc-listing.txt
grep "conf" ~/sysop-lab/etc-listing.txt | head -8
df -h | grep -v tmpfs
VERIFY
Record the line count from wc, two example lines from the grep, and the non-tmpfs filesystems shown by df.

Task 4 — Build one useful administrative pipeline of your own (at least two pipe stages):

# Example starting point:
du -sh ~/* 2>/dev/null | sort -hr | head -5
VERIFY
Write your full command and the resulting output.
Part C · Process and Resource Monitoring

Task 5 — Capture process and resource information:

ps aux | head -8
free -h
df -h | head -6
pgrep -a bash | head -5
VERIFY
Record total/used/available memory from free, the filesystem with the highest Use% from df, and one bash process PID from pgrep.

Task 6 (written, not graded here) — In 2–3 sentences, explain foreground vs. background (&) execution, and name the commands to list and bring back background jobs. Try it yourself: sleep 30 &, then jobs, then fg %1.

Scratch space only — transfer your real answer to the worksheet.
